A close match to a standing design choice here: the learner is a full LLM agent with its own deliberation, not a scripted role (`services/learnerTutorInteractionEngine.js`), and the human-learner pilot (`services/pilotStore.js`, `public/pilot/index.html`) and the `/ms-play-tutor` / `/ms-tutor-remote` skills already let a human step into one role of a dialogue while the rest of the cast is AI-played. AgentForge's four-role rotation is a concrete template for extending that beyond the tutor/learner dyad — a human could take any one of several roles in a drama's cast while the others run as usual, which is a different axis of variation than the current single-swap pilot mode.
