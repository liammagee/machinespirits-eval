# Recover a transportable learner substrate

The completed learner-profile/world deconfound failed its prospective 80% gate:
persona recovery was 13/20 (65%) and world recovery 15/20 (75%). That cohort is a
sealed negative result. Repeating it, rewriting the same personas after seeing
the errors, or relaxing the threshold cannot unblock the publication holdout.

The replacement substrate must begin with an independently authored account of
the learner's stable conduct commitments: what the learner presses, concedes,
forgets, protects, or refuses across materially different subject matter. World
costume and vocabulary are applied only after that account is frozen. Human
reviewers then verify that the crossed realizations preserve the intended
learner differences without importing the source world's evidence schedule.

Any later model-backed cohort must be prospectively balanced across learner and
world, freeze its recovery instrument before generation, and classify both
learner identity and world as co-visible diagnostics. This card carries no paid
run authority and makes no human-learning claim.

2026-08-30 Codex: Dropped as a near-term route after the human-work review. It
would spend scarce independent human authoring on stabilizing synthetic persona
identity after the prospective 65%/75% gate failure, while the stronger current
direction uses harness-owned public state for causal tests and real learners for
pedagogical outcomes. This is a portfolio decision, not evidence that a
world-independent synthetic learner substrate is impossible; the failed cohort
remains sealed and the proposal remains available as P3 history.
