# The pedagogical figure ontology

Sanctioned by the user 2026-08-06 ("solidify the conceptual base"; include
the seven levels in the paper). Source material: the living log's
vocabulary entries of 2026-08-03 — the meso-level unit, classical figures,
dialogue games as guarded programs, macro-theory as generative grammar,
Aristotelian episode effects.

## Scope

1. One note stating the seven-level ladder (wording; rhetorical
   construction; pedagogical figure / interactional move; dialogue game;
   character; trace-level relational effect; episode effect), the
   two-name rule (figure descriptive, move operational), and the reserved
   word (mechanism = supported mediator only).
2. An artifact map: every existing instrument placed on its level (cards,
   quiet cards, exemplars, licence, dose ladder, detection editions,
   registers and their fidelity gate, worlds and schedules, the
   dramatic-derivation stage, cast layer, rubrics).
3. Ten-field figure records for the six causally proven playbook entries
   plus the robust-native lost thread, with outcomes citing the closed
   arc cards and §6.24.
4. Paper §7.13 carrying the canonical short statement, with a revision
   entry (v3.0.267). No new empirical claims anywhere; the section's
   status line says so and names its anchors.

Out of scope, stated to stay small: no config-level figure registry (the
note is the registry until one earns its keep), no new figures, no
game-level experiment — level 4's missing crossed test is recorded as an
open observation, not scheduled work.

## Result (2026-08-06, same day)

Note and §7.13 shipped as scoped. The ladder places the programme's
evidence exactly — causal proof at level 3, formalism without crossed
tests at level 4, instrumented but bounded effects at levels 6–7 — and
names the recurring error class (reading one level's instrument as
another level's result: costume as treatment, manner as move,
critic-visible form as learning, instruction as capacity). Forward rule
recorded in the note: new figures enter via the living log's eleven-step
admission procedure, only against a priced deficit.
