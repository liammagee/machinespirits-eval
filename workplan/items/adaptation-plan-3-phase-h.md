Phase R narrowed this phase before it started: H2 (second family) was
answered by the opus seat run — opus makes the endgame stake-split unaided,
so the holes are family facts, not model-general. What remains is H1: can
one worked example teach SONNET a move that instruction alone never
produced? The standing target is the demand move (seize the deadline as a
test): 0 of every arm, both worlds, card present or not — including w033
where the demand card fired 5/5 and still converted nothing.

## Design (2026-08-02)

`TUTOR_STUB_CARD_EXEMPLARS=1` appends a worked example of the SHAPE to the
demand card (generic content, explicit transpose-don't-copy instruction,
and a named anatomy: deadline accepted, verdict priced in named evidence,
the check assigned to her, the tutor staking something on the outcome).
Cards version travels in every switch trace (`mc-v1` vs
`mc-v2-exemplar-demand`); no pooling across versions.

Baseline arm: v4-live (v4 trigger + qd-v1 detector, w030, k=3) — the first
arm where the demand card actually fires at the tenant's t2 (v4 hears her
ultimatum; the composer masking at the release turn remains, measured not
relieved). H1 arm: identical + exemplars. Scored by sol move-tagger with
the standing rulings; the gate reads on t2 (the demand's card-covered
moments) per the verbatim gate above.

## Results (2026-08-02): v4 fires live 3/3; GATE H takes the boundary branch

**v4-live baseline**: the demand card fired at t2 in all three dialogues
(triggerVersion v4-crossworld-demand in-trace) — detection is closed
live. Delivery: 0/3 (tagged slow_down ×2, backtrack); arm raw 10/18,
profile otherwise as the q2 family (t8/t9/t10 3/3 raw; t4 simplify tags
in the standing ruling-1 family).

**H1 exemplar arm**: t2 delivered 0/3 again — every t2 reply was the
release composer's fallback (this turn carries a scheduled clue, so the
composer owns delivery when the draft fails release checks; the
delivered-level gate reading is confounded there, recorded plainly). The
draft-level reading (recovery-candidate payloads, both arms): none of
the three exemplar drafts makes the FULL move — none accepts her
deadline outright, prices the verdict in named evidence, AND assigns the
check. **Gate H therefore takes its second branch: the demand move is
recorded as a generation boundary for the sonnet family** — instruction,
timing, detection, and now a worked example all fail to produce it,
while opus produced its sibling (the stake split) unaided at R4.

Recorded WITH the boundary, because it is measurable movement: the
exemplar shifts the drafts toward the shape. All three H1 drafts put the
decisive missing evidence to her as a question ("does it show water
reaching the mark, or just that the shower ran?") where the baseline
drafts state procedure ("no path, no verdict yet"); one engages her
clock directly ("I hear the clock. One minute, one fact"); and the
delivered openings under exemplars carry deadline-acceptance language
seen in no other arm ("Eight it is — I'll meet your clock, but the
verdict still has to earn it"). The shape is being approached
beat by beat; the full move does not come. Both halves are paper-grade
per the plan's own gate sentence. Arm raw 12/18 vs baseline 10/18 —
elsewhere the arms behave alike.
