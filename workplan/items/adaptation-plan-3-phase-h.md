Phase R narrowed this phase before it started: H2 (second family) was
answered by the opus seat run — opus makes the endgame stake-split unaided,
so the holes are family facts, not model-general. What remains is H1: can
one worked example teach SONNET a move that instruction alone never
produced? The standing target is the demand move (seize the deadline as a
test): 0 of every arm, both worlds, card present or not — including w033
where the demand card fired 5/5 and still converted nothing.

## Design (2026-08-02)

`TUTOR_STUB_CARD_EXEMPLARS=1` appends a worked example of the SHAPE to the
demand card (generic content, explicit transpose-don't-copy instruction,
and a named anatomy: deadline accepted, verdict priced in named evidence,
the check assigned to her, the tutor staking something on the outcome).
Cards version travels in every switch trace (`mc-v1` vs
`mc-v2-exemplar-demand`); no pooling across versions.

Baseline arm: v4-live (v4 trigger + qd-v1 detector, w030, k=3) — the first
arm where the demand card actually fires at the tenant's t2 (v4 hears her
ultimatum; the composer masking at the release turn remains, measured not
relieved). H1 arm: identical + exemplars. Scored by sol move-tagger with
the standing rulings; the gate reads on t2 (the demand's card-covered
moments) per the verbatim gate above.
