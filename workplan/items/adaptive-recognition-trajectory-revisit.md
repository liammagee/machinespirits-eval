# Conditional recognition-trajectory revisit

The later local-control findings do not rescue the May recognition-by-trajectory
hypothesis. They use a different intervention and a different outcome. This
card exists only so a future decision to revisit the original construct cannot
quietly borrow support from the state-timed move programme.

Reopening requires a substantive reason to expect a different answer: for
example, a revised construct, better-aligned temporal outcome, or a design that
separates prompt recognition, tutor architecture, and learner substrate without
changing all three at once. That reason and the new estimand must be written
before choosing cells or inspecting fresh outcomes.

This is not a current recommendation to rerun the null. It carries no model-call
authority, and a future null must close the branch without instrument shopping.
