Preserve the report's longer-range recommendation that the experimental unit
may eventually need to move from isolated typed actions to whole dialogue games:
multi-turn structures with branches, commitments, repairs, and exits.

The card is intentionally blocked. The current evidence is causal at the move
level, while the dialogue-game ontology is a representational formalism without
a crossed game-level effect. A future design must begin from a measured deficit,
hold models, world, resources, and move inventory constant where possible, and
cross materially different executable game policies against an appropriate
matched control. Recognition, charisma, role reversal, peripeteia, or ensembles
are candidate design vocabularies, not pre-certified treatments.

No game should run merely because the architecture can express it. No result
may be promoted to a human-learning claim without the separate human pilot.
