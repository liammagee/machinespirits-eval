# Lay-audience rewrite of the adaptive tutor hero demo

The first hero-demo draft was too suggestive, wordy and opaque for its
purpose. The purpose is marketing: show, clearly and simply, how the
adaptive tutor mechanism improves a given dialogue.

The rewrite (`notes/poetics/2026-09-01-adaptive-tutor-hero-demo.html`)
keeps one story: the Rowan Flat turn-10 stuck moment, the two delivered
replies, the 5/6 vs 0/6 conduct contrast, the three-step mechanism, the
four-state move repertoire, and the forced-wrong-move test. Research
apparatus the lay reader does not need (proof DAG walkthrough, frozen
instrumentation atlas, delivery-guard history) stays in the 2026-08-29
research note, which the demo cites as its evidence anchor.

All numbers inherit from the 2026-08-29 note and Paper 2.0 §6.23–§6.24;
no new claims, runs, or aggregates.
