Restart the staged integration of the tutor-stub and cell-based adaptation
lines from their current evidence boundary. Tutor-stub crossed tests support
resistance-matched pedagogical actions, including the flat/bored result; the
cell-side register studies establish signal-contingent switching but do not
establish improved learner outcomes, and the edged-register follow-up leaves a
small possible cost unresolved.

The programme therefore treats register as the realization of an action, not
as the action itself:

`public resistance observation -> adaptation warrant -> pedagogical action -> compatible register -> public realization -> outcome`

## Programme

0. Reconcile stale workplan states and remove only clean, contained worktrees
   and branches. Port or archive unique historical inputs before deleting them.
1. Define the shared typed boundary between resistance observation, warranted
   action, register compatibility, realization fidelity, and public outcome.
2. Normalize a bored learner and a frame-defiant learner into the tutor-stub
   profile-contract, observability, and zero-call discrimination machinery.
   Treat counterexample hunting, goalpost shifting, mockery, and substantive
   objection as distinct observable behaviours rather than one moralized
   `objectionable` trait.
3. Change composition so the pedagogical action is selected before register.
   Keep irony and sarcasm experimental, work-directed, uptake-closing, and
   suppressed for protected affect or comprehension repair.
4. Run a proof-DAG crossed design: matched versus mismatched action, warm/plain
   versus edged realization, and a same-treatment repeat. Measure the actual
   warm baseline before sizing any paid block.
5. Transfer a result to the cell harness only through the existing claim-
   transfer rule: destination-world re-scoring or an explicit home-world
   caveat. A null or negative result closes the relevant hypothesis.

No paid call is authorized by this card. Each empirical stage still requires
its own frozen registration, executable endpoint preflight, committed GO note,
and explicit human approval.
