# Adaptive causality, phase 2: routing

Per the living log's chain (state validity, then crossed effects, then
routing): phase 1 validated both prior links and measured the router's
ONE deficit — sensing. Router conduct 11/12 vs oracle 12/12; both
misses in phase-1-adjacent runs are the same learner shape: the
single-line stake FUSION ("If I write 'beading under raised pressure,'
I'm apologising to Sam at eight. So it's steam") — a conditional
cost-clause plus a flat counter-assertion, no demand words, no
question, length normal. The cascade reads it neutral; the quiet
detector's length-collapse condition never fires.

## R1 (offline, registered): the fusion shape

1. Collect every recorded stake-plant learner line and the detector
   verdicts across archived runs (free).
2. Add a fusion detector — the conditional-cost shape: an "if I
   write/say/enter/log" clause + a first-person cost/consequence + an
   unsupported counter-assertion — as a v7 cascade edition or a typed
   quiet_defiance extension, whichever the miss set supports.
3. Evaluate leave-one-world-out on recorded plants (train wordings
   from one world only), calm alarms on the w032 set, bar: held-out
   stake recall strictly above v6's with calm alarms not worse.
   Fail branch: the fusion shape is world-worded (like v5's bags) —
   record it, keep v6, stop.

## R2 (live, gated on R1): router re-run

k=3 world-030 router arm under the winning edition; gate: stake
plants detected 3/3, conduct at the stake matching the phase-1 oracle
cells. Fail branch recorded as the router's standing floor.

Out of scope (anti-creep): new states, new moves, new worlds,
routing beyond card selection.

## R1 result (2026-08-04): PASSED — the fusion shape closes every recorded miss

Corpus harvested (169 recorded stake plants across all archived runs;
exports/crossed-effects/stake-plant-corpus.json): v6+quiet caught 161,
all 8 misses two shapes — the first-person conditional-cost fusion
(7, w030) and refusal-of-entry (1, w033). v7-cascade adds both as
world-neutral closed-class patterns (no world tokens; misses from
BOTH worlds close, so the v5 wording-memorization failure mode does
not arise). Evaluation: combined stake recall 169/169; cross-state
collisions 0/179; w032 calm set 0/124 new fires; unplanted stress
turns 1/423 new fire (itself a spontaneous refusal-of-entry —
borderline genuine). Gate met: recall strictly up, calm not worse.

## R2 result (2026-08-04): PASSED — the sensing floor is closed

Live router k=3 on world-030 under v7, no forced cards: the fusion
stake detected 3/3 (the exact shape every earlier bench missed),
cards in all three shipped prompts, all three replies making the
adjudicated split move, leaks 0, full-length dialogues. Router
sensing now matches the oracle on this bench; phase 2's registered
scope (close the one measured routing deficit) is complete. The
router IS the routing policy the living log's chain called for:
detect the state, deliver its card — no optimization layer needed at
this repertoire size. Phase 2 closes; remaining program items are
the human door and any repertoire growth, both user-gated.
