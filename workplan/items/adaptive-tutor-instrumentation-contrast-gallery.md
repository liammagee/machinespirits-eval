# Instrumentation contrast gallery

Extend the adaptive-tutor HTML explainer with a larger array of transcript-level
with/without cases. Reuse the frozen A/B harness so that the learner turn,
world, speaking model, and recorded guard set remain fixed while one advisory
block changes.

## Acceptance

- Hold the bare reply constant and let the reader switch among context
  continuity, evidence window, learner classifier, learner DAG, human scaffold,
  and first-draft contract replies.
- Provide at least three shared learner moments so the gallery shows a visible
  change, convergence/no-effect, and an over-specified effect rather than one
  cherry-picked success.
- Label these as frozen-context tutor-reply comparisons: later learner turns are
  counterfactual and no human-learning or free-running outcome claim follows.
- Separate plan-aware mechanical audits from plan-blind or schedule-shown
  judgments; never use the former as an independent quality score.
- Reuse the existing swimlane/comparison grammar and keep plain-language glosses
  explicitly editorial.
- Add a second cross-device atlas that places each instrument at its causal
  location, names the exact with/without comparison class, and distinguishes
  visible-record re-encoding, hidden-state injection, untimed manner, and
  state-timed action without treating unlike rulers as one leaderboard.
