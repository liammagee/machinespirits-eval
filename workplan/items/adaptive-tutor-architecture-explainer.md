# Adaptive tutor architecture explainer

Turn the adaptive-tutor programme's detailed results into a light, public-facing
HTML explanation of what changed between the May null and the current bounded
causal-control result.

## Acceptance

- Show the architectural sequence without implying that the later apparatus
  rescues the original recognition-by-trajectory hypothesis.
- Mine adjudicated transcript artifacts and state an explicit excerpt-selection
  rule; never present an illustrative rewrite as recorded evidence.
- Use the established tutor/learner swimlane grammar and add clearly labelled
  plain-language glosses where the authored world or experimental terms obscure
  the conduct.
- Make the proof DAG inspectable as a choice between a tempting unsupported
  shortcut and the evidence path the system actually permits.
- Keep sensing, selected action, delivered reply, immediate conduct, transfer,
  and human learning visibly distinct.
- Reuse the completed `transcript-render-standard` conventions. Do not open a
  second renderer project unless this report exposes a concrete missing shared
  interface.

## Closeout

The report reuses the completed swimlane grammar without opening a duplicate UX
card. Its central distinction is explicit: the May null concerns trajectory
modulation, while the later crossed experiments identify a forced-action effect
on delivered tutor conduct at planted simulated states. Transfer and human
learning remain separate, open questions.
