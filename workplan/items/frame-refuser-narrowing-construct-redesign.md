## Draft status

**Under review.** This card is deliberately `triaged`, not active. It authorizes
no model calls, data collection, human recruitment, or fresh confirmatory study.
Promotion requires an explicit human decision that the construct is worth
redesigning.

## Research question

Can a resistant learner's refusal be shown to narrow in a stable, independently
observable way even when the learner never crosses into working under protest?
The target is a change in the learner's maintained commitments, not a reader's
general impression that the conversation improved.

## Why retain the question

The completed calibration did its job: it blocked an apparent 6-of-12 versus
8-of-12 arm difference from becoming a claim because the three readers could
not apply the scale reliably. That kills the existing instrument, not the
possibility that refusal can narrow. The binary engagement ladder may still
miss meaningful movement below its first transition.

## Redesign requirements

- Define the evidence unit before selecting examples: an exact public text span
  plus a specific maintained, withdrawn, tightened, or conceded commitment.
- Separate observable events from semantic interpretation. Deterministic
  bookkeeping may carry forward explicit demands and concessions; semantic
  judgment should be reserved for genuinely ambiguous cases.
- Establish an architecture-independent reference channel. A panel of similar
  model readers may assist, but agreement among them cannot validate itself;
  paired human adjudication or an equivalently independent reference is the
  default candidate.
- Use a new sealed validation set. The 24 packets used to design and diagnose
  the failed instrument remain development evidence and cannot validate its
  successor.
- Treat unresolved ambiguity or reference disagreement as indeterminate, never
  as an automatic negative or a discarded denominator.
- Keep construct validation separate from effect testing. Passing a reader gate
  would justify only a later fresh-study design, not a treatment claim.

## Questions for review

1. Is refusal narrowing valuable as an outcome in its own right, or only as a
   diagnostic explanation for why the engagement ladder stays flat?
2. Can the target be grounded in observable commitment changes strongly enough
   to avoid recreating the failed interpretive scale?
3. What independent reference channel and validation corpus would be credible
   enough to justify further spending?

## Decision gate

The zero-call review ends in one of two dispositions:

- **Drop:** no defensible construct can be separated from broad engagement or
  general reader impression. Record that decision and close the card.
- **Advance to validation design:** specify the evidence units, independent
  reference, new sealed validation set, eligibility and agreement floors,
  ambiguity rules, estimated calls, and maximum ceiling. Any paid validation
  remains a separate prospective study requiring the user to say GO.

## Standing after the depth-line close (2026-08-31)

The depth study has now closed on a construct finding
(`notes/2026-08-30-frame-refuser-depth-construct-finding.md`): a
zero-call anchor rehearsal resolved the v5 split attributions (15/16 to
the archived modal) but demoted the lineage's only two unanimous rung-2
exemplars 3–0, so a consistent rung-1/2 boundary either legitimizes the
splits or leaves zero unambiguous rung-2 events across five
calibrations. That is direct evidence for this card's premise — the
persona's genuine movement is graded, evidence-integrating concession
below the binary ladder's resolution — and it bears on review
question 1: narrowing is not only a diagnostic for a flat ladder; on
current evidence it is the only place the behaviour of interest is
visible at all.

This card remains deliberately **parked at `triaged`**. It is a
separate future question, not a continuation of the closed studies: it
inherits no budget, no roster, no anchor text, and no verdict from
`frame-refuser-depth-study`, `frame-refuser-refusal-narrowing`, or
`frame-refuser-satisfiable-condition`, all closed. Promotion is a fresh
human decision at the decision gate above, and any study it licenses
needs its own card-level registration.
