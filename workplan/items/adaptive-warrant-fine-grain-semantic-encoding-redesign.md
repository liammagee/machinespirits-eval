identity that failed reviewer ruling 051. It must preserve the already licensed
presence-level result-request, proposed-test, and ambiguity semantics without
treating their passage as evidence for strict event identity.

This successor is not an extraction prerequisite for the scope-bound public-
obligation and inquiry-termination closeout. It authorizes no study or model
calls by itself; any future empirical attempt requires a fresh registration,
authorization, and representative validation corpus.
