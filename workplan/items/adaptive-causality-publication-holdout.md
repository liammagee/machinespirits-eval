Preserve the living log's publication-closeout recommendation without treating
the present evidence as launch-ready. The holdout must select only two or three
primary claims and cross the learner substrate with worlds rather than repeat a
persona/world pairing. It must also include two model families and retain the
fixed, random or yoked, wrong-action or scrambled, and oracle comparators needed
by the exact claims selected at registration.

The completed learner-profile/world deconfound did not license this step:
persona recovery was 65% and world recovery 75%, both below the prospective 80%
bar. Unblocking therefore requires a new, independently justified learner
substrate whose state can be validated across crossings; merely rerunning or
relaxing the failed threshold is forbidden. The human state/move validation
card must also complete before any claim-bearing launch.

This card records a future experiment only. It carries no paid-run authority,
does not revive the May recognition-slope hypothesis, and cannot support human
learning or deployment claims.

visible while its failed transportability prerequisite is preserved honestly.
