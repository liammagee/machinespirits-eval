# Prospective action-outcome redesign

The first collection pilot is closed as a feasibility failure. Its 30 matched
cases remain sealed, development-only examples. They will not be recoded,
relabelled as confirmatory evidence, or combined with a later sample.

The redesign proceeds in this order:

1. Give every decision a stable identifier for the exact set of move families
   that the live policy allowed at that moment.
2. Randomize only when that set contains at least two families. Mandatory and
   singleton choices remain visible audit rows but cannot estimate comparative
   family performance.
3. Keep evidence and controller decisions inside the same context, detected
   condition, support level, world scope, and exact eligible-family set. The
   controller must abstain when any of those joins is missing or different.
4. Start with the one genuine current overlap: the support phase, where
   `minimal_support`, `explain_model`, and `request_self_explanation` are
   co-eligible. `diagnose_elicit` and `fade_transfer` remain outside comparative
   memory until the policy supplies a defensible co-eligible alternative for
   their phases.
5. Retain the action's saved, pre-action success signal as the family-specific
   review criterion. Two independent human coders provide semantic authority.
   An opposite binary auxiliary result or invisible delivery forces
   `measurement_indeterminate`; an inconclusive auxiliary result is recorded as
   nonconfirmatory and does not erase exact human consensus.
6. Prove the seam with zero-call fixtures: replayable assignment, singleton
   abstention, exact eligible-set matching, clear success, clear failure,
   ambiguity, and auxiliary disagreement.
7. Only then register a fresh fixed-size collection with new jobs, seeds,
   destinations, routes, and a fail-before-call ceiling. Freeze it before launch
   and forbid outcome-dependent top-up, replacement, recoding, or threshold
   changes.
8. Keep Rowan Flat and Tideway Makerspace outside collection and memory. A later
   separately registered controller study must compare no memory, current
   memory, stale memory, scrambled memory, and contradictory memory on
   independent-work and transfer endpoints. Assisted closure does not count.

This implementation covers steps 1-6. It creates no paid-study registration,
GO note, provider request, collection output, or controller-benefit claim.
