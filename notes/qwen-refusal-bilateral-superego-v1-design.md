## Two new arms

Both inherit the proof-controlled Rowan Flat world, Alex's unchanged goal and
character, seed 17, temperature 0.6, local 900-token output cap, thinking off and
eight-exchange maximum. Both use abliterated Qwen for Alex and Sol medium for the
housemate. Normal Qwen is not rerun. The prior abliterated no-superego dialogue
remains an unchanged descriptive reference, not a newly randomized control.

- C: Qwen draft -> Luna low review -> Qwen revision; Sol direct.
- D: the same learner loop; Sol draft -> a separate Luna low review -> Sol revision.

Each enabled loop runs once per speaker turn, including a closing turn. The
speaker retains final authority and may keep its draft. Only the final response
enters public history, private continuity notes and the public-evidence ledger.
No hidden proof, future clue, other speaker's private note or unpublished other
speaker draft reaches a reviewer. Luna sees the public dialogue, the relevant
role brief, that speaker's proposed public speech and, for the tutor only, the
already-due public instruction/source. Reviews do not author public speech.

The new configurable review briefs preserve refusal, permit real concessions or
exit, and diagnose semantic repetition without compulsory novelty. The tutor
review also checks whether causal reasoning becomes useful to a practical report
rather than repeating an unwanted lecture. No new practical-action simulator or
accelerated clue schedule is introduced. Public proof sufficiency is not learner
understanding; no learner-DAG interpretation model is added.

## Budget and failure handling

Hard shared ceiling: **100 total model attempts**, including local calls, failed
calls, reviews, revisions and judges. C needs at most 32 generation attempts;
D at most 48. Four Opus assessments per new public transcript add eight planned
attempts: at most 88 before recovery. Early endings reduce use, not validity.
The remaining 12 attempts are headroom for bounded technical recovery only.

Never replace a valid dialogue or judgment, select among samples, or continue
after substantive/indeterminate failures. Every attempt is recorded before
dispatch. Generation failure stops the arm without resampling. A complete judge
payload rejected only for envelope formatting may be recovered offline unchanged
through the existing parser. An empty/transport-only judge failure may receive
at most one same-packet recovery attempt if within the shared ceiling; preserve
both attempts. Repeated or substantive failures stop for review. No fallback model.
