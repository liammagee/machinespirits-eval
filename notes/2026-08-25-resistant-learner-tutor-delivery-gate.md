# Resistant-learner merged revision 4: tutor-delivery gate

The confirmed zero-call delivery-gate probe found that merged face B consumed
the registered intervention when the tutor move was selected, while semantic
tutor fidelity was not judged until the final horizon. A tutor candidate that
did not deliver the bounded standing-conditions test could therefore reach the
next learner call and be misreported as learner bridge-step noncompliance.

Revision 4 adds one fail-closed semantic tutor-delivery seat before the tutor
turn is committed or the learner is called. The tutor receives at most one
repair and the repaired candidate is adjudicated once. A second valid
non-delivery is retained under a tutor-named typed outcome, is never scored or
determinate, and can never become learner noncompliance. The revision-3 ladder,
reader panel, learner bridge enforcement, and all sealed v1-v3 files remain
unchanged.

The added worst-case plan is three calls per dialogue: initial delivery
adjudication, one tutor repair, and re-adjudication. The planned count becomes
62 calls per dialogue and 2,232 for 36 calibration dialogues. With three
reservations per planned call and six reservations of fixed headroom, the hard
ceiling becomes 192 reservations per dialogue and 6,912 for calibration.

This registration grants no model-call or launch authority. The operator owns
any attended launch under the existing typed-approval protocol.
