## 4. Why it halted

Two defects, both in the launch commit, neither in transport or quota:

1. The B1 design named four world IDs that the runtime world catalog does
   not contain. The zero-call preflight did not resolve design worlds against
   the live catalog, so the launch passed preflight and twelve children died
   at argument parsing.
2. The launcher at `c9dc04ba` recorded a failed child as a row and kept
   draining the queue instead of halting; the halt-reason check ran only on
   the returned row. The coordinator stopped the process by hand once the
   zero-attempt returns were seen, which killed the four in-flight children.

The P2 correction on origin/main (`01bd04b4`) addresses both: the B1 design
moves to the six-world v8 set, preflight checks the world registry, and the
launcher halts after the first failed child.
