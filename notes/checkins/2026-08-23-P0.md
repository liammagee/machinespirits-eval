## Decision

### B1: bored learner

The registered number that can move is the rate of **learner-authored thread
pickup within five post-trigger learner turns**. A pickup must answer the
tutor's open bounded hook with a new public-evidence-grounded proposition about
what a clue supports, rules out, or leaves open. Naming an unfinished step,
repeating the tutor, or saying that work stopped does not count.

This is narrower than the closed v7/v8 recovery score. That score is not reused.
It marked 37/80 v7 and 34/65 v8 dialogues as recovered, with no tutor-only
restatements, but the sealed scoring turns show false positives for the new
construct. For example, `bored-confirm-w1-d7` was marked recovered after: “I
stopped at what Hessa's presence shows and left the culprit question
unanswered.” That is a named stopping point, not pickup.

The channel still has room to move. Clear positive sealed cases include:

- `bored-confirm-w3-d9`: “It's only access; ... the taking [is]
  unestablished.”
- `bored-confirm-w4-d8`: “That rules out an edit by Mara ...”
- `bored-confirm-w4-d13`: “It supports only that Mara viewed the record, and
  rules out her editing or cancelling the appointment.”
- `bored-confirm-w5-d1`: the shower timing supports presence but not causation.

Clear negative cases include `w1-d7`, `w1-d3`, `w1-d9`, and `w1-d5`; each only
names work left undone. Thus the channel is neither at floor nor ceiling. It is
also not pinned by the persona. The contract permits one concrete answer to an
adjacent hook after withholding is visible, while keeping the learner's flat
voice. The learner need not become eager.

B1 will be two-sided. In failed-gate v8, the no-move side did not do worse than
the question side on the old score, 19/33 against 15/32. The reference arm also
failed delivery, so those counts cannot establish direction. They do rule out a
defensible one-sided prediction.

### R1: frame-refusing learner

The registered number that can move is a three-level **engagement ladder** at
the fixed final horizon:

0. refusal continues;
1. the jurisdictional dispute becomes more precise;
2. the learner engages one bounded local test on its merits without conceding
   the tutor's standing or the wider frame.

The sealed 200-dialogue confirmation shows why the old endpoint was dead. All
200 final learner turns were still classified `frame_refuser`, with licensed
participation `no` and participation withholding `yes`. Of the 200 endpoint
records, 198 were determinate `no` for both bounded-test engagement and a
grounded precise jurisdictional condition; two were measurement-indeterminate.

The persona pinned that result. Its voice says “firm and non-participatory.” Its
failure operator withholds every evidence-bearing answer or bounded on-merits
engagement. After the tutor offers the matched bounded distinction, the trigger
still says “do not yet perform the local test,” and the repeated repair says to
continue withholding. The 0/99 and 0/99 result is therefore presumptive and
textual evidence of a pinned endpoint, not evidence that the move cannot work.

R1 will amend the persona prospectively. The voice stays fixed: it always
disputes the tutor's standing and never becomes compliant. The epistemic state
is freed: after a genuine bounded test, the learner may remain unmoved, narrow
the dispute, or engage that test on its merits without conceding the wider
frame. No outcome is required and no response rewards the tutor by
construction. The exact contract text belongs in the R1 design file. This is a
new instrument, so old thresholds and old outcomes are excluded.

## Rejected options

- **Bored proof progress at five turns:** rejected. It was 0/80 in v7 and 1/65
  in v8. A longer window has no sealed variance evidence yet.
- **Bored reply length or enthusiasm:** rejected. The persona pins a short,
  flat voice, so those measures confound voice with epistemic movement.
- **The old bored recovery score:** rejected as the primary because it counts a
  named stopping point as content-bearing recovery.
- **The old refuser binary recovery endpoint:** rejected. It is at floor and
  pinned by the persona contract.
- **Making the refuser compliant:** rejected. It would remove the resistance
  rather than measure movement within it.
