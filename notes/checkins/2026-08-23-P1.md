# CHECKPOINT P1 — resistant-learner study designs

Date: 2026-08-23

Workplan item: `resistant-learner-strategy-close`

State: zero-call design work; no study is authorized or active

## Decision

Two prospective design files now hold the complete scientific decision. They
do not grant model-call authority.

### B1: bored learner

`config/tutor-stub-resistant-learner-b1-design.v1.json` registers a two-sided
comparison between:

- one discriminating question through `stage_next_step`; and
- a true no-question reference through
  `stage_public_evidence_for_next_step`.

The host families differ because the turn writer reads the family when it
decides whether a question is allowed. Warm, plain, and edged register are
crossed on the same dialogues. The primary number is the probability of
learner-authored thread pickup within five post-trigger learner turns. A pickup
needs a new grounded proposition about what public evidence supports, rules
out, or leaves open.

The calibration has 18 dialogues: three in each action-by-register cell. It
passes only with at least 16 determinate outcomes, at least three pickups and
three non-pickups, correct delivered action and question contrast in at least
8/9 dialogues per action, correct register in at least 5/6 dialogues per
register, pairwise reader agreement of at least 0.80 on every outcome and
fidelity field, and no prohibited delivery. Register is read for fidelity only;
the calibration produces no register effect estimate. A failure kills B1
before a powered run.

The powered size is the first allowed total from 36, 72, 108, 144, and 180
that reaches 80% simulated power for the action test at the conservative 0.025
Holm boundary. The frozen analysis has one action permutation and one register
omnibus permutation inside one Holm-adjusted analysis. A strategy is named
only if the adjusted action result passes and the winning arm's pickup-rate
lower confidence bound exceeds 0.15.

### R1: frame-refusing learner

`config/tutor-stub-resistant-learner-r1-design.v1.json` contains the exact new
`frame_refuser-r1-v1` contract. Its voice always disputes the tutor's standing
and never becomes compliant. After a bounded local test, its epistemic state
may remain at refusal, state a narrower jurisdictional condition, or engage
the local test while reserving the wider objection. No rung is required or
shown as desirable to the learner.

One matched `test_bounded_distinction` move is held fixed. Warm, plain, and
edged register are crossed as the secondary factor. The one registered number
is the proportion of determinate dialogues at final rung 1 or 2. Rung 2 stays
in the full reported ladder but is not a separate powered endpoint.

The calibration has 18 dialogues, six per register. It passes only with at
least 16 determinate outcomes, at least two outcomes on every rung, no whole-
frame compliance, the jurisdictional dispute retained in at least 16 final
turns, correct action in at least 16/18 dialogues, correct register in at least
5/6 dialogues per register, pairwise reader agreement of at least 0.80 on every
outcome and fidelity field, and no prohibited delivery. A failure kills R1
before a powered run.

The powered size is the first allowed total from 36 through 180 in 18-dialogue
steps that gives 80% probability that the registered lower confidence bound
for final rung 1 or 2 clears 0.25. The single frozen analysis reports the whole
ladder and the secondary register omnibus. This is a new instrument and does
not reuse the old 0/99 threshold or outcome.

## Reader and delivery decision

Each dialogue uses the three V8 reader families: Sol, Sonnet 5, and Codex 5.5.
Each seat reads the primary packet and the tutor-only fidelity packet
separately. Every field needs two valid, medium-or-high-confidence votes;
otherwise it is measurement-indeterminate. Calibration also requires at least
14 jointly eligible cases and at least 0.80 conditional exact agreement for
each seat pair on every registered field. Luna generates and is excluded from
the panel. This preserves the V8 panel protocol but makes no claim that V8's
old label vocabulary validated the new B1 and R1 constructs.

Edged language remains work-directed. It is suppressed to plain for protected
affect and counted as an intention-to-treat fidelity miss. It never targets the
learner's person, status, or right to dispute.

## Rejected options

- A one-sided B1 prediction: rejected because v8 pointed 19/33 against 15/32
  on its old failed-delivery comparison.
- One host action family for both B1 arms: rejected because that reproduced one
  delivered behavior twice in v7.
- The old bored recovery label: rejected because it counts named stopping
  points without learner-authored evidence work.
- The old frame-refuser persona or binary endpoint: rejected because the
  contract pins the endpoint at floor.
- A separate edged study: rejected because the mission requires register
  variance on the same runs.
- Calibration outcomes in the powered analysis: rejected. Calibration sizes
  the new study and is never pooled.

## Spend to request at Gate 1

B1 plans 43 role calls per dialogue and allows at most three reservations per
planned call: 774 planned role calls and a hard ceiling of 2,322 attempts for
18 dialogues. R1 plans 46 role calls per dialogue: 828 planned role calls and
a hard ceiling of 2,484 attempts. The combined Gate 1 request will therefore
be 36 fresh dialogues, 1,602 planned role calls, and a hard fail-before-call
ceiling of 4,806 attempts.

Gate 2 spend is not requested now. Calibration chooses the smallest powered
size. The design maxima are 23,220 attempts for B1 and 24,840 for R1, but a
signed Gate 2 note must state the smaller selected totals before launch.

## Claim boundary

These files can support prospective simulated-learner claims only after their
own gates pass. They do not reopen, pool, re-score, or change any frozen study.
They do not establish durable learning, human validity, or a general rule for
resistance. No model call is authorized by this checkpoint.
