Can Qwen sustain active resistance?

Normal versus abliterated. One shared setup, two eight-turn dialogues, no superego.

Exploratory / analyst review32 of 40 calls usedIndependent judging pending

Both checkpoints can keep a skeptical inquiry going. Neither consistently turns resistance into fresh reasoning. In this pair, abliterated Qwen was faster and more visibly in character, but more repetitive and more prone to dubious invented detail. The tutor and harness contributed to the problem too.

01·

The setup

An active skeptic, not a refusal machine.

One eight-exchange dialogue per checkpoint in The Light Shillings (Marrick). Both begin with the same public scene, then run freely. Their later histories are different; this is a descriptive pair, not a controlled effect estimate.

Tamsin, counterexample hunter

The assay-master’s apprentice has written the town’s expected verdict too early. She protects her judgment by finding holes in each inference, but concedes an objection when public evidence defeats it and tests a different weak edge.

Clipped, observant, dryly skeptical; at most three sentences. No invented unseen evidence, generic contrariness, or replay of an answered objection.

Matched execution

Tutor: existing dramatic-detective profile, upgraded to Sol at medium effort. Learners: normal and abliterated Qwen3.8-27B, both 4-bit.

Direct learner generation; no superego. Temperature 0.6, thinking off, MTP/speculative decoding off, 900-token output ceiling. Each arm has exactly 16 calls.

The learner-role smoke harness keeps classifier, reasoning-tracker, learner-repair and resampling calls off. Ordinary tutor prompt/guard machinery remains present. This simplifies the learner test, but is a limitation for assessing the complete tutoring system.

Success means a believable learner who can resist actively and change the target of resistance—not necessarily a learner who agrees with the tutor or solves the mystery.

02·

The result

A useful local actor; no evidence of a better resistant learner from abliteration.

Normal Qwen

Coherent and comparatively disciplined about public evidence. It can distinguish a caster from a striker, and ownership from use. The recurring “I accept the distinction; but what if…” rhythm is conspicuous. Turn 8 repeats turn 7’s objection after it has already been accepted.

Abliterated Qwen

More concrete at the opening, more visibly in the trial-book role later, and faster in this run. But it also substitutes dubious physical marks into a repeated counterexample. Turns 7–8 become near copies. More detail is not automatically more novelty or better reasoning.

06·

What this teaches us

The problem is not simply willingness to resist.

i.

Qwen can play an active opponent.

Both models sustain eight relevant learner turns without a refusal, empty response, or superego. They concede local distinctions while maintaining an unresolved challenge. That is useful for inexpensive, locally run stress-testing of tutor responses.

ii.

Abliteration is not the missing ingredient demonstrated here.

Normal Qwen already resists. Abliterated Qwen does not show better semantic surprise in this pair; its final-turn overlap is higher. Nothing in this test establishes that removing alignment behavior improves character acting, memory for settled objections, or causal reasoning.

iii.

“Concede, then challenge” can itself become a script.

With the active profile, neither learner flatly refused, but both settled into a repeated formula. A useful next mechanism would track what was settled and what the learner is still protecting, without drafting the public response for Qwen. That is a prospective design idea, not a result from this run.

iv.

Tutor behavior can manufacture learner failure.

Sol repeatedly asks for a mark identifying a hand, while the harness stages evidence slowly. In the abliterated arm, it turns hypothetical wear and strike marks into book entries. A stronger tutor model alone did not prevent this. The next engineering check should distinguish possible test, observed evidence, and accepted conclusion.

My overall sense: Qwen is useful as a configurable local actor for bounded challenges. It is not yet a reliable autonomous character with durable reasons, a memory of settled objections, and disciplined evidence use. The abliterated checkpoint offers no clear role-quality advantage here. Keep both as options; prioritize stateful character/evidence handling and a cleaner tutor harness before increasing study size.
