## Purpose

A prospective semantic-v5 operation remains possible as a **new conceptual
replication** of the dramatic-form mechanism question. It would not recover,
reproduce, validate, replace, or directly compare with the historical v4
matched-pair result (3 positive / 6 null; lift 0.333). That result's current
provenance boundary is recorded separately in
`notes/poetics/2026-08-27-edra-m3-historical-v4-reproducibility-audit.md`.

The v5 estimand would be the proportion of valid matched-prefix comparisons in
which the peripeteia-only branch shows a semantically adjudicated tutor-provided
public mechanism, learner representation change, and learner action while the
routine/none controls remain clean. Two independent, high-confidence judgments
must quote valid before/after evidence. Ambiguity, invalid evidence, or judge
disagreement is `measurement_indeterminate`, not a null; recognition in a
control invalidates causal interpretation rather than counting against the
treatment.

## What it could show

- whether new simulated dialogues exhibit treatment-specific semantic
  representation change under the v5 instrument;
- the positive, null, measurement-indeterminate, control-leak, and invalid-
  coverage rates;
- whether failures concentrate at the tutor mechanism, learner representation
  change, or learner-action stage; and
- whether the result repeats across the prospectively registered anchors,
  draws, models, and judge panel.

It would remain evidence about critic-rated dramatic form in a simulated dyad.
It would not establish human or durable learning, deployed-tutor effectiveness,
model-weight adaptation, or the correctness of the missing historical v4
aggregate.

## Readiness boundary

Do not start a claim run now. The registered clean-anchor set remains incomplete:
D50 and D53 are the required core, D42 is a calibration boundary, and the
outcome-blind existing-evidence screen of D54-D57 qualified no third anchor.
`qualified_third_anchor` is therefore null and `claim_gate_ready` is false. A
two-anchor run would silently narrow the denominator and is prohibited.

Reconsider v5 only if the project explicitly prioritizes this bounded
dramatic-form question. Before any model-backed operation, prospectively:

1. qualify a third clean anchor from control behavior without viewing treatment
   outcomes;
2. freeze the matched-prefix design, models, judge panel, seeds, sample size,
   thresholds, outcome dispositions, and spend ceiling; and
3. preserve create-once transcripts, semantic judgments, item gates, aggregate,
   and hashes so the new result is independently reaggregable.

Until those conditions hold, the option is parked. No v5 operation was started
while recording this note.
