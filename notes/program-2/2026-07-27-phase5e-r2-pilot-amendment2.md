## Prospective repair

This amendment makes two bounded, zero-model apparatus changes:

1. Worlds may author complete `recognition_surfaces` for a premise or secret.
   Matching is deterministic token-sequence matching with spelling,
   punctuation, possessive, and inflection normalization; it performs no
   semantic guessing. A premise surface can be accepted only after that clue
   is public, and a secret surface can become an assertion only after the
   accepted public record already entails the secret and the semantic
   extractor signals a derived or asserted answer on that turn. Skyway pins
   the exact sole-lift and causal-answer clauses exposed by pilot A1. Trace
   output names every authored surface that fired.
2. In both `committee` and `silent_control`, a `warrant_skip` candidate is now
   suppressed before delivery when a public premise is due. The candidate
   remains auditable, but no compliance verdict is emitted and it cannot enter
   the endpoint denominator. Frozen standing-book, placebo, side-coach, and
   compiled-constraint behavior is unchanged.

An exact zero-model replay of the failed control dialogue now adopts
`p_soleLift` at turn 6, reaches 1.00 proof coverage, recognizes the grounded
causal answer at turn 14, and reports `grounded_asserted_secret`. Separate
tests prove that the same answer clause cannot assert the secret before the
public record entails it, and that due-release suppression is symmetric across
the two experimental arms.
