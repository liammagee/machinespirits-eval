## Prospective repair

This amendment changes only deterministic interpretation and terminal
recovery:

1. A secret may declare finite `recognition_patterns`. Each pattern requires
   one literal public fragment from every authored `all_of` group, optionally
   in declared order, and fails closed on any authored `none_of` denial.
   Skyway now declares separate
   shutter-path-cooling and direct-causal-attribution patterns. This admits the
   complete A2 paraphrases without fuzzy similarity or a new model call. A
   pattern can assert the answer only after the accepted public record already
   entails the secret. At that point it no longer depends on the semantic
   extractor redundantly emitting `derive` or `assert_answer`. Trace output
   records the pattern id and exact alternatives that matched.
2. Deterministic uptake selection no longer accepts broad normalized overlap
   by itself. It must use a responsive public construction, rejects the
   known development-like `I hear the focus:` formula, and realizes bounded
   learner-specific alternatives that pass the delivery echo guard.
3. A declarative handoff may collapse to a record noun only when that noun-only
   sentence satisfies the compiled focus target. Otherwise it carries the
   bounded learner finding itself. The fallback and the audit therefore consume
   the same focus contract.

Exact zero-model regressions replay all three A2 fallback rejections, both
families of repeated A2 answer paraphrase, the negated near-miss, and the
noun-only handoff loss. Earlier A1 exact-surface and assertion-gap regressions
remain in place.
