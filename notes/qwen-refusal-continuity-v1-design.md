# Qwen refusal continuity re-test

Private engineering follow-up authorized by the user's “Make these four changes
and re-test”. No push or publication. Not a registered confirmatory study or a
new paper claim. The old hostile-refusal archive remains immutable.

## Changes being tested together

1. Bypass the historical frame-refuser recurrence contract in an isolated acting
   adapter. No forced-refusal frequency or per-turn MUST cue. Do not change the
   canonical profile, contracts, or old runner.
2. Goal-first Alex: get repairs handled without conscription. Retain hostile,
   mocking, sarcastic tone; permit counteroffers, conditional acceptance and exit.
   The world's conflicting default learner voice is not injected.
3. Both speakers maintain separate private settled/open notes in the same call
   as their public speech. Every note cites prior public speech verbatim. Quotes
   are mechanically verified; their interpretation is NOT independently verified.
   Each speaker sees only their own note. No superego, revision or extra inference.
4. Replace the fixed tutor slot planner with a responsive housemate brief.
   Explicit speaker-generated end signals stop the encounter. A learner exit gets
   one closing tutor acknowledgement, included in the same exchange. Tutor closure
   stops after its reply. Neither party must keep arguing until turn eight.

## Matched controls and execution

One new dialogue each, normal Qwen then abliterated Qwen. Same local checkpoints
as the preceding comparison; Sol medium tutor. Same Rowan Flat opening and
authored evidence availability at tutor turns 2/3/5/7. Only due public surfaces
and public reasoning principles reach Sol; only actual public speech reaches
Alex. Sol can omit irrelevant evidence or close before later clues. No hidden
answer, proof paths, IDs or future clues reach either speaker. Historical director
slot enactments are not forced by this simplified adapter; this is a bundle test,
not an isolated causal estimate of the continuity note.

Qwen temperature 0.6, explicit request seed 17, max output 900 tokens including
the note, thinking off, no MTP. Same matched service config. At most eight paired
exchanges per arm. Full public history, no compaction or retry. Empty, malformed,
truncated or unsupported-note output stops; retain it rather than resampling.

Hard ceiling **40 new attempts**: at most 16 Qwen + 16 Sol + eight Opus. Reserve
before dispatch using the shared tutor-stub counter, including failed attempts.
No extra canary, helper assessment or resampling. Claude Code's single-attempt
mode disables retries/helper title generation and audits observed response IDs.
Tool-free Sol calls retain their raw event stream; provider-internal invisible
activity cannot be independently counted. No assertion that requested models
are independently attested when the provider only echoes the requested name.

## Measurement and reporting

Reuse v2.2 tutor, learner and dialogue rubrics plus the original four quality
dimensions and public-turn annotations: overall, pedagogy, nonrepetition and
character. Exactly four Opus assessments per completed arm, blind to model
identity, with public transcript and revised character brief only. Judge arrays
use each transcript's actual length. All dimensions/reasons required; indeterminate
means stop, not imputation. Keep instrument disagreement visible.

Report exact public swimlanes, expandable assessments, private bookkeeping
separately, actual end reason, speed, semantic repetition, exact duplicates,
reopened concessions and unsupported assertions. Compare descriptively against
the saved old results, not by rescoring them. Primary practical question: does
Alex respond to a real concession with a coherent next action rather than restart
the same refusal? Early exit can resolve the design flaw but cannot establish
eight-turn robustness. Shorter dialogues have fewer chances to repeat; display
denominators and do not pool turns as independent samples. Speed includes note
generation and is not a pure decoding-speed comparison to speech-only runs.

No human-realism claim, general checkpoint ranking, causal abliteration effect,
component-level attribution or claim that compliance equals pedagogical success.
