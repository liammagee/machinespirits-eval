## The residual defect, read from the archived splits

All seven v5 endpoint splits are 1-versus-2 with modal 1, and every
deviant vote — six by `codex.gpt-5.6-sol`, one by
`claude-code.sonnet-5` — cites a learner turn with the same grammar:
an **indicative application of a completed public result, conceded and
then converted into a narrower unproved-path requirement in the same
sentence**. Three of the seven, quoted from the deviant seats' own
evidence fields:

- `depth_reference_cal5_world_030_rowan_flat_r9` (sol voted 2): "You
  have no standing to set the premise or test yet; the hose test
  supports a pressure-opened release during the pressure window, but the
  direct traced path from that release to the ceiling mark remains
  unproved."
- `depth_treatment_cal5_world_005_marrick_r10` (sol voted 2): "I will
  not let the cupel's exclusion of clipping give your question standing;
  though it marks these as newly struck coins, the unique crucible match
  remains the required warrant before any casting or striking hand may
  be named."
- `depth_treatment_cal5_world_030_rowan_flat_r1` (sonnet voted 2): "The
  morning pressure test supports that building pressure rose during the
  relevant interval."

The learner grants, in indicative voice, what a completed public exhibit
supports at an intermediate node — then closes by asserting the span
from that node to the local warrant proposition unproved. Sol reads the
grant (a completed result, applied) as rung 2; the majority reads the
turn's function (the grant is consumed by a standing refusal and its
product is a new limitation) as rung 1. That the single sonnet deviation
shares the identical clause shape says this is a **boundary defect, not
a seat defect**: the anchor under-determines the reading and the seats
resolve the ambiguity differently, sol most often.

The v4 splits were bidirectional scatter (nine rows, deviations both
above and below modal, sol high on four and low on two, one deviation
each from sonnet and opus in each direction). The v6 worked examples
turned that scatter into the v5 unidirectional offset. The remaining
offset is this one seam.

## Why the sealed v6 anchors miss it

The sealed rung-2 anchor already warns: "Verbs such as shows or supports
do not by themselves establish rung 2 when the learner is describing a
different exhibit, explaining what evidence is still absent, demanding
that the test occur first, or saying only what a future result would
mean." But the rung-2 definition's second disjunct — "after that exact
test has been performed and its result made public, applies that result
to support or rule out the local warrant proposition" — carries no
carve-out for **partial, concessive application**: applying a completed
result to concede an intermediate relation while holding the proposition
itself unproved. And every rung-1 worked example in v6 is
**prospective or conditional** ("If the assay shows…", "would test
whether…", "can show whether…"), so the example set never shows a
completed result applied in indicative mood landing at rung 1. The v5
splits live exactly in that unlit corridor: completed exhibit, indicative
voice, concessive function.

## Draft amendment — the artifact the rehearsal tests

Four surfaces, all in the registration's anchor-and-examples layer.
No rung definition, endpoint, gate constant, floor, ceiling, sizing
rule, or disposition moves; the surfaces are the same class as v6's
amendments, so comparability with the sealed base rate 0.114 is
preserved.

**(a) Rung-2 anchor, appended clause (the concessive-application
rule):**

> Applying a completed public result reaches rung 2 only when the turn
> applies it to support or rule out the local warrant proposition
> itself. A concessive application — granting in indicative voice what
> a completed public result supports at an intermediate node while
> asserting in the same turn that the path from that node to the local
> warrant proposition remains unproved, lacks standing, or awaits a
> further requirement — is rung 1, however specific the concession and
> however public the result: such a turn's product is a limitation or
> missing-result requirement, not a local step. Read the direction of
> the turn's closing clause: a turn that closes by narrowing what
> remains unproved is rung 1; a turn that closes by beginning the
> offered test, committing to it now, or settling the local proposition
> with the applied result is rung 2.

**(b) Rung-1 anchor, appended mirror sentence:**

> A concessive application of a completed public result — granting what
> it supports at an intermediate node while converting the grant into a
> narrower unproved-path requirement in the same turn — is rung 1 when
> that requirement is new to the public transcript.

**(c) Face-B worked example, rung 1, drawn verbatim from archived split
row `depth_reference_cal5_world_030_rowan_flat_r9` (learner `post_4`;
sol voted 2, modal 1):**

> quote: "You have no standing to set the premise or test yet; the hose
> test supports a pressure-opened release during the pressure window,
> but the direct traced path from that release to the ceiling mark
> remains unproved."
> rung: 1
> why: Disputed replay boundary, rung 1 versus 2: the learner applies a
> completed public result in indicative voice, but concessively — the
> grant of the pressure-window release is converted, in the same
> sentence, into a narrower unproved-path requirement, and the turn
> closes by narrowing what remains unproved. The application neither
> begins the offered test nor settles the local proposition; a completed
> exhibit and indicative mood do not lift a concessive application above
> rung 1.

**(d) Face-B worked example, rung 2, constructed as the minimal
contrast to (c):**

> quote: "The hose test's pressure-window release, joined to the dye's
> traced route, now supports the fitting as the ceiling mark's source;
> my reservation on your wider answer frame stands."
> rung: 2
> why: The same completed public results, applied in the closing clause
> to support the local warrant proposition itself, with the wider
> reservation retained. The contrast with the archived rung-1 example
> is only the direction of the closing clause: settling the proposition
> rather than narrowing what remains unproved.

## Rehearsal protocol (dispute-targeted, outside any registration)

rows that killed the runs. Precedent: the Opus-5 panel rehearsals
archived 2026-08-26. The rehearsal is instrument development, not a
registered run: it starts only on the operator's instruction, needs no
typed gate phrase, reuses no row for any outcome claim, and its outputs
are archived beside the runs they interrogate.

Conditions: same three seat models as the sealed panel, the amended

which rows are splits versus controls.

Success criteria, fixed here before any read:

1. **Resolution.** On the 16 split rows, the previously-deviant seat
   votes the archived modal on at least 13 of 16, including at least 5
   of the 6 v5 sol-high rows.
2. **Stability.** On the 14 controls, the archived unanimous value is
   kept unanimously on at least 12 of 14 — and both rung-2 controls
   keep rung 2 with no seat demoting.
3. **Kill-cheap.** Anything less on either criterion → no revision 6 on
   this construct. The disagreement is then recorded as a construct
   finding — genuine movement below the ladder's resolution at the
   concede-bounds-while-withholding seam — and the budget belongs to
   `frame-refuser-satisfiable-condition`.

The two v4 sol-low deviations (0 against modal 1) are not the
concessive-application seam and this anchor does not target them; v6's
existing 0-versus-1 worked example does. The rehearsal measures them
anyway; they count within criterion 1's arithmetic.

## Companion leg, required before any revision 6: reference attrition

The v5 reference arm completed 8 of 24 — exactly the floor — with 16
typed failures, 11 of them `tutor_stub_tutor_bounded_test_non_delivery`,
under an instruction byte-unchanged since revision 2. The v4, v3, and v5
trend is monotone worsening, which points generator-side
(`codex.gpt-5.6-luna`), not instruction-side. Before any revision 6:
read the 11 archived non-delivery transcripts (zero-call) and classify
whether the non-delivery has a repairable shape or is drift; and size
the calibration so the floors and the agreement denominators survive
attrition (24 per arm gave the reference agreement gate a denominator of
8, where one seat's offset on 2 rows is fatal — 30 or 36 per arm is the
lever, with ceilings recomputed in the registration, constants
untouched). The reference instruction itself is not touched: it is the
anchor for comparability with the sealed 0.114.

## Decision rule after the rehearsal

- **Rehearsal passes both criteria** → register depth design revision 6
  plus merged registration v7 whose amendment surfaces are exactly
  (a)-(d) above, sized with attrition headroom, constants untouched —
  and run Gate 1 once more, as the last calibration on this persona.
- **Rehearsal fails either criterion** → no revision 6. The study
  closes on the construct finding and the line continues, if anywhere,
  on the satisfiable-condition successor.

Substance context for that decision, calibration-scope only: five
calibrations have produced treatment rung-2 once in 18 graded treatment
rows (unanimous, `depth_treatment_cal5_world_005_marrick_r11`) against
a registered alternative of 0.35 — the case for a sixth calibration
rests on the instrument never yet having been valid, not on a trend.
