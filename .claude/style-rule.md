Plain-language rule for this reply (repo standing rule — edit .claude/style-rule.md to change it):

1. Short plain words over Latinate ones. use not utilise; show not demonstrate; enough not sufficient; more not additional; then not subsequently; before not prior to; help not facilitate; get not obtain; need not require; try not attempt; end not terminate; start not initiate; about not approximately/regarding; many not numerous; part not component; change not modify; check not verify; find out not determine; give not provide; do not perform; happen not occur; but not however; so not therefore; make sure not ensure; now not currently; first not initial; last not final.
2. Verbs, not nominalisations. "fixing it" not "the implementation of the fix"; "latency dropped" not "a reduction in latency was observed"; "we added X" not "the introduction of X".
3. Banned constructions: "not merely X but Y"; "X is not just A, it is B"; "operates at the level of"; "what X actually is"; a closing aphorism that restates the point as an epigram; "it is worth noting that".
4. Banned surface: arrow chains (A -> B -> C); two or more parentheticals on one claim; coined compounds (X-adjacent, X-shaped, X-licensed); arc slogans and project shorthand used in chat instead of saying the thing.
5. Never write "honest" or "honestly", in any register.
6. Answer what was asked and stop. One pointer per omitted topic, not an inline detour.
7. Count the words before sending. Over ~250 on a bounded question means cut it, don't polish it. Drop the tables, insight blocks and bold pseudo-headings first — structure is not brevity.
8. Say borrowed vocabulary in English on first use. "Each version of the tutor", not "each arm". "Broken rules", not "clusters". The drama words (part, tactic, clue release, actorial realization) and the logic words (proof-DAG, warrant frame, proof debt) are load-bearing and stay as they are.
9. Never paste a code identifier as an explanation. Translate it, or give the identifier once in parentheses after the English.
