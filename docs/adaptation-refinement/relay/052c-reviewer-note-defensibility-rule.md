## The standing design rule (banked for future reader instruments)

**A slot enters an agreement test only if a competent reader could
defend their answer from the transcript alone. Engineering slots
stay extractor-only.**

Applied to this arc: the fine-grain gate inherited the FULL
extraction record into the two-reader agreement identity — including
slots the prose cannot decide, such as requested-field lists and
choices among near-twin catalogue entries. Two careful readers can
disagree on those without either being wrong about the transcript;
scoring that disagreement as reader failure builds guesswork into
the floor. That is the deeper reading of the ruling-051 FAIL: the
diff profile (targets 54, actions 29) sat mostly in exactly these
slots. The presence-grain re-registration is the repair — its three
gating slots (did the learner ask for a result; did the learner
propose a test; is the case genuinely ambiguous) are each defensible
from the transcript alone.
