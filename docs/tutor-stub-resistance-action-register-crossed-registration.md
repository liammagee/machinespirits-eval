# Resistance–Action–Register Proof-DAG Crossed Study — Frozen Design

**Frozen:** 19 August 2026.
**Status:** design HOLD; no model call, pilot, main block, or consumer switch is
authorized.
**Workplan item:** `resistance-action-register-integration`.

The machine-readable design authority is
`config/tutor-stub-resistance-action-register-crossed-registration.v1.json`.

## What is frozen

The study intervenes only after the first eligible public resistance signal in
an otherwise identical proof-DAG dialogue prefix. It then crosses:

1. a **matched** or **mismatched** typed pedagogical move;
2. a **plain**, **warm**, or **edged** realization; and
3. two independent **same-treatment repeats**, A and B.

The learner instruments are only `bored` and `frame_defiant`, which passed the
prospective held-out public-axis gate. Profile identity cannot trigger the
intervention: the runtime must observe one unambiguous public axis first.

The action mismatch is a clean swap. A bored signal receives either the
matched `ask_discriminating_question` move or the mismatched
`test_bounded_distinction` move; frame defiance receives the reverse. No third
or deliberately harmful action is invented.

Register remains downstream of the assigned move. `plain` and `warm` are exact
positive controls. In the edged arm, an assigned discriminating question is
realized sarcastically and an assigned bounded-distinction test ironically.
Thus the edge follows the move being realized, not a learner label. Face threat
is excluded.

## Trigger and safety boundary

Each source dialogue is frozen at its first eligible resistance moment. Every
treatment branch shares the exact public history, public evidence, proof-DAG
state, authored-release state, source/runtime, and model route up to that
point. Branching happens only after the trigger.

No treatment is assigned when both resistance axes fire, comprehension repair
is active, protected affect is exposed, or content-bearing uptake is already
visible. An edged assignment lasts one tutor turn and closes after uptake. If a
protected condition appears before delivery, the safety path uses plain
realization, records non-adherence, and never rerolls or selects another model
output.

The intervention must remain study-only and opt-in. The legacy tutor path stays
authoritative everywhere else; this registration does not authorize the Phase
3 shadow decision to become a general runtime consumer.

## Outcomes and contrasts

The primary endpoint is public, profile-specific resistance recovery:

- for boredom, a content-bearing answer to one adjacent concrete question
  within the next learner turn, without permission seeking or mere assent;
- for frame defiance, engagement with the bounded test on its merits or a more
  precise frame dispute within two learner turns.

No model judge defines this endpoint. Secondary outcomes are objective
proof-DAG debt change, newly supported public premises, grounded closure and
time to closure, plus action/register realization and safety-override rates.

The analysis order is fixed. Trigger specificity, treatment fidelity, and the
A/B same-treatment stability gate come first. Only then may the study read:

- matched minus mismatched action under the non-edged controls;
- edged minus plain and edged minus warm within the matched move;
- the action-fit × edge interaction; and
- downstream proof-DAG outcomes.

An edged register cannot rescue a failed action-fit result. A positive tonal
claim must agree against both plain and warm. Same-treatment instability blocks
a treatment claim. A null or negative result closes that hypothesis rather
than licensing threshold or register tuning.

## Why the design is not executable yet

The current Phase 3 runtime is deliberately shadow-only. The repository does
not yet have the study-only trigger override, move-to-host-action realization,
post-action register assignment, exact-prefix branch runner, or endpoint
assembler needed to execute this design. Those are engineering prerequisites,
not details that may be improvised in a GO request.

The main factorial also cannot be sized honestly from the previous profile
study: that study measured whether learner states appeared, not the recovery
rate under matched warm/plain action. A prospective 24-dialogue baseline pilot
is therefore frozen first: three fresh prefixes per profile, matched action,
plain and warm realization, and two same-treatment repeats. It is excluded
from the confirmatory main block and may inform only sample size, the
meaningful-effect floor, and the repeat-stability tolerance.

After that pilot, a prospective amendment must freeze those numeric gates, the
main sample size, exact command, create-once destination, endpoint certificate,
and model-attempt ceiling before any main-block outcome exists. Neither pilot
nor main block may launch without its own digest-bound GO request and explicit
human approval.

## Evidence boundary

The established normative/descriptive-DAG, `low_agency`, and `overconfident`
results remain read-only conservation authorities. The earlier resistant-
profile negative result is not overwritten by the later public-axis instrument
pass. Even a positive crossed result would initially be a tutor-stub/home-world
finding; transfer to cells requires the existing claim-transfer rule.
